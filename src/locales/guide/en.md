# User guide

## Getting started
Comic Fast has three areas: the **page list** on the left, the **canvas** (the page you are editing) in the middle, and the **resources** on the right with the **properties** of the selected object below them.
1. Pick a template in the *Panels* tab.
2. Drag images into the panels.
3. Add balloons and text.
4. Save (`Ctrl+S`) and export.

## Pages
- **＋** creates a page, **⧉** duplicates it and **🗑** deletes it. Drag the thumbnails to reorder them.
- *Page → Custom size…* lets you choose any size (mm, cm, inches or pixels) or a common format such as A4, manga or webtoon.
- With the page selected (click an empty area) you can change its background color.

## Panels
- The *Panels* tab has full-page templates, single panels and shapes (circle, diamond, hexagon…). Click or drag them onto the page.
- Select a panel to move, resize or rotate it. Under *Shape → Polygon* you can move its vertices; double-click an edge to add a vertex.

## Images
- *File → Import images…* (or drop files onto the window). They appear in the *Images* tab.
- Drag an image onto a panel to fill it. With the panel selected, the **Layers** section lets you cover, fit, rotate and center the image.
- The *Included* tab ships ready-to-use images and silhouettes; you can search them by name.
- *Image filters* adjust brightness, contrast, color, blur and more. “Tint” paints silhouettes in a single color.

## Balloons and text
- *Balloons* has speech, thought, shout, whisper and narration. Double-click a balloon to type.
- In the properties you change font, size, colors and alignment. Drag the tip of the tail to point at the character and the white handle to curve it.
- *Text* has text boxes, titles and sound effects.

## Patterns, textures and gradients
They can be added as page objects or as **layers** inside a panel (with a panel selected). Use them for backgrounds, shading or speed effects. Rotate and scale them by dragging the handles on the canvas. **Gradients** show two points on the canvas: in a linear gradient they mark the start and the end; in a radial one, the center and the radius. Every color has a **transparency** slider.

## Order, lock and selection
- *Edit → Bring to front / Send to back* changes which object sits on top.
- **Lock** (`Ctrl+L`) prevents moving or deleting an object by accident.
- `Ctrl+A` selects all, `Ctrl+D` duplicates and `Delete` removes. `Ctrl+Z` undoes and `Ctrl+Shift+Z` redoes.

## Saving and exporting
- *Save* creates a `.cfast` file with the whole project (images included).
- *Export page as PNG* saves the current page; *Export all as PDF* or *CBZ* saves the whole comic.
- Everything happens on your computer: nothing is uploaded to any server.

## View, theme and language
- *View* has zoom, grid and snap to grid. `Ctrl+0` fits the page to the window.
- Top right: **A** changes the interface text size, the sun/moon switches between light and dark theme, and the **globe** changes the language.
